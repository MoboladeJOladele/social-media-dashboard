The Top BG Pattern for the Dark Theme is 1440 x 240.


To get a circle in Figma, you divide the width by 2 (but the width and the height must be the same). While you divide the height by 2 to get a pill.
Quick mental shortcut

Circle → radius = width ÷ 2

Pill → radius = height ÷ 2
